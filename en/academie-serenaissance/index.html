A specialized, advanced learning space that equips doulas with solid, ethical foundations grounded in physiology, in New Brunswick, Quebec, across Canada and in France. Founded by Sabrina Chavanel, a doula in Campbellton, N.B.

Why Académie Sérénaissance was created

A basic doula training necessarily covers a lot of subjects, and the fourth trimester takes up only a small place in it. Sabrina wanted to go straight to the heart of this period. The Académie was born from a deep need to give structure to, and raise the standard of, the practice of perinatal support workers.

It is a space for sharing knowledge, built on safety, rigour, absolute respect for physiology and deep postpartum support, so that every practitioner feels equipped, competent and fully confident in her work with families.

The Académie's courses

Advanced Fourth Trimester Support and Deep Postpartum Care

About 35 hours, 5 modules and 33 chapters, reserved for certified doulas: postpartum physiology, matrescence, perinatal mental health, the Golden Month, the newborn, breastfeeding, legal framework, perinatal grief.

Discover the postpartum doula training →

Breastfeeding Peer Supporter

About 8.5 hours and 6 chapters, open to everyone, no prerequisites: lactation, latch, common difficulties and when to refer, listening skills and helping relationship (counselling support).

Discover the breastfeeding peer supporter training →

Both courses are 100% online, for learners in New Brunswick, Quebec, the rest of Canada and France. The content presents the care pathways, procedures, assistance and legal framework of each of these places. The Académie's certificates confirm completion of a course: they are neither regulated professional titles nor diplomas recognized by a ministry or public body. The course content is available in English.

To get a feel for the Académie's style: the free preview of the first chapter and the mini-guide "5 Postpartum Warning Signs" (PDF).

Our approach to teaching

Flexible learning

100% online modules so you can progress at your own pace, combining rigorous theory and practical cases.

Ethics and safety

A framework based on best clinical practices and the "Observe and Refer" principle.

Mentoring as needed

You are never alone: throughout your course, Sabrina supports you personally, by email or video call.

Frequently asked questions

What courses does the Académie offer?

Two 100% online courses: Advanced Fourth Trimester Support (about 35 hours, 5 modules, 33 chapters), reserved for certified doulas, and the breastfeeding peer supporter training (about 8.5 hours, 6 chapters), open to everyone, no prerequisites.

Who are they designed for, and where can I take them?

For learners in New Brunswick, Quebec, the rest of Canada and France, and elsewhere in the world. The content presents the care pathways, procedures, assistance and legal framework of each of these places, and the practicum kit documents were designed to be used there. The course content is available in English.

Can I see a preview before I enrol?

Yes: the free preview shows you the beginning of the first chapter, and the mini-guide "5 Postpartum Warning Signs" can be downloaded for free.

How does enrolment work?

You fill in the form (with your doula diploma for the fourth trimester course), pay securely with Stripe, then create your password. You then receive a welcome email with a button to confirm your address.

Is there an app?

Yes! The Académie installs like an app on your cell phone, tablet or computer, without going through the App Store or Google Play. On iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". On Android: Chrome's ⋮ menu, then "Install app". On a computer: the install icon in the address bar of Chrome or Edge. You can also simply take your course in your browser.
