Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Académie Sérénaissance courses. Another question? Write to me 💕

The courses

Which courses does the Academy offer?

Two 100% online courses: the Advanced Fourth Trimester Support course (about 30 hours, 5 modules, 33 chapters), reserved for certified doulas, and the Breastfeeding Peer Supporter course (about 8.5 hours, 6 chapters), open to everyone, with no prerequisites.

Who are they designed for, and where can I take them?

For English speakers in New Brunswick, Quebec, the rest of Canada and France, and elsewhere in the world. The content presents the care pathways, procedures, financial supports and legal framework of each of these places, and the practicum kit documents were designed to be used there. This is the English version of the courses.

What is the format?

Chapters to read at your own pace, on a computer, tablet or cell phone, with figures, case studies, printable tools and videos to listen to. Each chapter ends with a quiz; for the fourth trimester course, each module ends with an exam, and the peer supporter course ends with a final exam.

How does progression work?

The next chapter opens once you have passed the chapter quiz (80%). The module exam opens when all the quizzes are passed, and the next module opens once the exam is passed. Your progress is saved in your account: you'll find it on all your devices.

What if I fail an exam?

Don't panic! First reread the chapters where you made mistakes, then retake the exam. The questions change from one attempt to the next: you really have to master the material, not memorize answers.

Will I be supported?

Yes! Personalized mentoring is offered as needed throughout your course, by email or video. You can book a time directly from your account.

Can I see a preview before registering?

Yes: the free preview shows you the beginning of the first chapter, and the mini-guide "5 postpartum warning signs" can be downloaded for free on the Courses page.

The Breastfeeding Peer Supporter course

Who is it for?

Doulas and support people, moms who want to support other moms, and anyone who cares about breastfeeding. No diploma is required.

What will I learn?

The role of the peer supporter and the ethics of peer support, how lactation works, latch and positions, common challenges and when to refer, listening and the helping relationship, and special situations such as expressing milk or returning to work.

Is this an official certification?

This is not a course of Public Health New Brunswick or of any other official program, and it does not confer a professional title or accreditation from a public body. It leads to the Sérénaissance Breastfeeding Peer Supporter certificate, valid for 2 years, which lets you join its network of volunteer peer supporters. It also prepares you to join an official program, if you wish.

What happens after the course?

Once you pass the final exam, your certificate of participation is mailed to you, then your account asks whether you'd like to join the Sérénaissance team of peer supporters. If so, you fill out and electronically sign your official form (photo, short bio, service areas, support methods): you keep a PDF copy. Sabrina checks your results and your profile, then publishes it on the "Our Breastfeeding Peer Supporters" page. Matches are managed by Sabrina and are always free and voluntary for moms.

How long is the peer supporter certificate valid?

2 years from the date of completion. To renew your status, you take a course update: at 50% off if you were trained at Sérénaissance, or at full price if you were trained elsewhere.

During the launch offer (until October 5, 2026 at noon, for the next 10 registrants), the peer supporter course is offered with registration for the fourth trimester course. As soon as your access is opened, you receive a "gift" email and it appears in "My courses".

Why the Académie Sérénaissance came to be

A basic doula training necessarily covers many subjects, and the fourth trimester takes up only a small place in it. I wanted to go straight to the heart of this period. The Académie Sérénaissance was born from a deep need to structure and elevate the practice of perinatal support people.

I wanted to create a space for sharing knowledge founded on safety, rigour, absolute respect for physiology and deep postpartum support. My goal is for every practitioner to feel equipped, competent and fully confident and autonomous in her practice with families.

"Guiding doulas toward a confident, rigorous and caring practice."
