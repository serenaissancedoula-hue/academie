      3. Important warnings
      🎁 Free peer support

      When the Académie matches you with a family, your peer support is entirely free and voluntary: no payment, donation, tip or purchase, and you do not offer your paid services to these families.

      💼 My professional practice

      In your professional practice (for example as a doula), with families the Académie has not matched you with, you are free to charge or not for your services, without ever presenting them as Sérénaissance peer support.

      🩺 My role and its limits

      You offer peer support: no diagnosis or medical advice, you refer to a health professional (811, midwife, nurse, doctor, IBCLC) and to 911 in an emergency. You respect the WHO Code: no promotion of infant formula, bottles or pacifiers.

      🌿 Product suggestions

      You only suggest a product (supplement, herbal tea, breast pump…) if a recognized training qualifies you to do so, within the limits of that training. These suggestions are your sole responsibility: the Académie is not responsible for them. You do not sell anything to matched families and you receive no commission.

      🛡️ Child protection

      The law requires you to report yourself any child you believe is being abused, neglected or in danger: Social Development in N.B. (1-833-733-7835), the Director of Youth Protection (DPJ) for your region in Quebec, 119 in France. Immediate danger: 911 (17 or 112 in France). You then inform the Académie.

      🔒 Confidentiality and communications

      Nothing families confide in you is shared or published, and you do not give advice about their situation in public groups. The photos they send you are used only to support them and are deleted as soon as they are no longer useful.

      📋 Monthly follow-up sheet

      Each month, you fill out a short anonymous follow-up sheet in your account (number of families and contacts, types of support, referrals). You receive a reminder in your account, in the app and by email. And if you go through a difficult situation, Sabrina is there for you in your Messages.

      🔁 Valid for 2 years

      Your peer supporter certificate is valid for 2 years from the date you passed. To stay on the team, you update your training: 50% off if you were trained at Sérénaissance (full price if trained elsewhere).

      4. My declarations
      Conflict of interest

      Do you have a connection (employment, sponsorship, commission, representation) with a company that makes or sells infant formula, bottles, pacifiers, breast pumps or other products related to feeding babies?

      Criminal record

      You declare that you have no criminal record incompatible with a support role with families and young children, and you agree to provide a background check if the Académie asks for one.
