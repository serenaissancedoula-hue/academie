Doula in Campbellton: Birth and Postpartum Support

I am Sabrina Chavanel, a doula, helping-relationship counsellor and naturotherapist, and a mother of 5. For 3 years, I have supported families in Campbellton and the surrounding area before, during and after birth.

  • Prenatal · birth · postpartum
  • Available 24/7 from 38 weeks
  • Video call or in person
Book my free discovery call Call 506-759-2044

A doula in Restigouche to see you through pregnancy, birth and the postpartum period

I offer a safe and warm space to expectant parents. Motherhood taught me something essential: we are not well prepared, and we are not supported enough. My training in the helping relationship gave me valuable tools to welcome the anxiety and stress so often present around the arrival of a baby. My support is non-medical: it does not replace the care of a midwife, nurse or doctor.

Attentive

I truly listen to you. I notice the small signs and the unspoken, and I adjust my support to what you need.

Respectful

Your body, your birth, your family, your choices. I inform you without ever judging, and I respect your pace.

Organized

Birth plan, hospital bag list, support circle, coming home: I help you prepare everything so you can breathe.

Frequently asked questions about my support

Do the meetings take place in person?

Individual sessions and birth preparation sessions can take place by video call or in person, depending on your preferences.

From when are you available 24/7?

From 38 weeks, you are my priority 7 days a week, 24 hours a day, and I am officially on call.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes: the Parent Support Package can be paid in 1, 2 or 4 payments, the Couple Support Package and Baby's Arrival Package in 1 or 2 payments.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellation is free before the first meeting. For a session booked in my calendar, cancellation is free up to 24 hours before. Details are in the terms of sale.
