Becoming a Breastfeeding Peer Supporter: who is it for?

The training is for…

  • doulas and perinatal support workers;
  • mothers who want to support other mothers;
  • anyone who cares about breastfeeding;
  • in New Brunswick, Quebec, elsewhere in Canada and France.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain simply how lactation works.
  • Support "hands-free" latching.
  • Recognize warning signs and know when to refer.
  • Listen and build confidence, without judgment.

Prerequisites: no diploma, just a computer, tablet or cell phone with Internet access, and about 8.5 hours at your own pace.

What the online breastfeeding training covers

  1. The role of the breastfeeding peer supporter

    Peer support, what a peer supporter does and does not do, ethics and peer support programs.

  2. Understanding lactation

    How milk is produced, the first days and weeks, and the signs that breastfeeding is going well.

  3. Latch and positions

    Skin-to-skin, baby-led breastfeeding, a good latch and "hands-free" support.

  4. Common difficulties and when to refer

    Common difficulties, warning signs, medications, substances and breastfeeding.

  5. Listening and the helping relationship

    Listening to understand, building confidence, respecting every journey and the mother's mental health.

  6. Special situations and next steps

    Expressing and storing milk, returning to work, school, breastfeeding in public, and taking care of yourself as a peer supporter.

Each chapter includes a tool, a case study, a practical exercise, a practice quiz and sources.

Assessment and peer supporter certificate

  • 6 practice quizzes and 6 practical exercises with answer keys.
  • A final exam of 24 questions (80% to pass).
  • A printed certificate of participation, mailed to your home (it is not available for download).

Good to know: this is not a training of New Brunswick Public Health or of any other official program, and it does not confer a professional title or accreditation from a public body. It leads to the Sérénaissance breastfeeding peer supporter certificate, valid for 2 years from the date you pass, which lets you join its network of volunteer peer supporters. It also prepares you to join an official program, if you wish.

After the training: the network of volunteer peer supporters

Once you pass the final exam, you fill in your peer supporter profile in your account (photo, short bio, areas served, ways of supporting) and sign your membership and consent form electronically. Sabrina checks your results and approves your profile: it then appears on the Our Breastfeeding Peer Supporters page.

Matches between a mother and a peer supporter are managed by Sabrina and are always free and volunteer-based for mothers. The peer supporter offers peer support: she does not replace a midwife, a nurse, a lactation consultant (IBCLC) or a doctor, and she refers when needed.

Are you a breastfeeding mother looking for support? Ask to be matched or call Sabrina at 506-759-2044.

Frequently asked questions

Who is it for?

Doulas and perinatal support workers, mothers who want to support other mothers, and anyone who cares about breastfeeding. No diploma is required.

Is it an official certification?

This is not a training of New Brunswick Public Health or of any other official program, and it does not confer a professional title or accreditation from a public body. It leads to the Sérénaissance breastfeeding peer supporter certificate, valid for 2 years from the date you pass, which lets you join its network of volunteer peer supporters. It also prepares you to join an official program, if you wish.

How long is the peer supporter certificate valid?

2 years from the date you pass. To renew your status, you take an update of the training: at 50% off if you were trained at Sérénaissance, or at full price if you were trained elsewhere.

What is the format?

Chapters to read at your own pace, on a computer, tablet or cell phone, with figures, case studies, printable tools and videos to watch. Each chapter ends with a practice quiz, and the training ends with a final exam.

When do I get access to my course?

After your file is verified, usually within 24 to 48 hours. You then receive an email, and your courses appear in My account.
