Online Doula Training, 400 Hours: From Pregnancy to Postpartum

Want to become a doula? The Académie Sérénaissance foundational training prepares you to support families before, during and after birth: 12 modules, 71 chapters, live workshops, 4 supervised practicums and mentoring, online and at your own pace. Live workshops are given in French, with your camera on at all times (unless the school's direction grants an exception); the self-paced modules are available in English too.

A doula training created in the field

In many trainings, the postpartum period and what families really live through are only skimmed over. Here, they have their full place. The training was created by Sabrina Chavanel, a doula, helping-relationship practitioner and mother of 5, together with an IBCLC lactation consultant and a midwife.

It is built on safety, rigour and ethics. You learn to recognize warning signs and to apply the principle of “Observe and Refer”, so that you arrive in the field ready to support families, who make themselves vulnerable when they call on a doula.

Who is this doula training for?

For you if you are…

  • someone who wants to become a doula, with no health diploma;
  • a mom, practitioner or support person who wants a solid, safe foundation;
  • comfortable learning in French or English, in New Brunswick, Quebec, the rest of Canada or France.

Prerequisites

  • Be 18 or older and read French fluently (modules also in English).
  • A device with Internet, a camera and a microphone. Your camera must stay on during live workshops, unless the school's direction grants an exception.
  • Be able to complete 4 in-person practicum follow-ups.
  • Before the practicum: first aid and adult and infant CPR.

What you will be able to do

  • Support pregnancy, birth and the first hours with confidence.
  • Offer comfort measures, positions and concrete emotional support.
  • Recognize warning signs: observe and refer.
  • Help parents make informed choices and collaborate with the care team.
  • Build your doula practice ethically.

Live workshops, supervised practicums and mentoring

  • Two live video workshops: a mandatory 2-hour breastfeeding workshop with Laurence Boulanger, clinical nurse and IBCLC lactation consultant, and a workshop with a midwife. Camera on is required for the whole workshop, unless the school's direction grants an exception.
  • 4 supervised practicums (140 h): four complete follow-ups with families, with your document kit.
  • 30 h of mentoring with the trainers and Sabrina, by email or video.
  • Practice of comfort measures and 2 simulations (prenatal meeting; family in a vulnerable situation).

Meet our trainers.

Assessment and certificate, delivered to your door

A quiz per chapter, an exam per module, filmed comfort measures, 2 simulations, 4 practicum follow-ups, a final case study and a final exam. On passing, you receive your Académie Sérénaissance doula certificate, printed and mailed to you. Suggested duration: 12 to 18 months · access for 24 months.

This certificate attests that you completed the training: it is neither a regulated professional title nor a diploma recognized by a ministry of education.

Live workshops are given in French. Also available in French: page en français.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a health diploma to become a doula?

No. The training is for anyone who wants to become a doula, with no health diploma. It also teaches you to recognize the limits of your role and to refer.

How long is the training?

The training is 450 hours. The suggested duration is 12 to 18 months and you have access to your training for 24 months.

Are there practicums?

Yes: 4 supervised practicum follow-ups (140 hours in total), done in person. Before the practicum, you need first aid and adult and infant CPR.

Are there live workshops?

Yes, two video workshops: a mandatory 2-hour breastfeeding workshop with an IBCLC lactation consultant, and a workshop with a midwife. Your camera must stay on for the whole workshop, unless the school's direction grants an exception.

Is the training offered in English?

Yes: the self-paced modules are available in English too. The live workshops (the breastfeeding workshop with an IBCLC and the midwife workshop) are given in French.

Will I be supported?

Yes. You have 30 hours of mentoring and can talk with the trainers and Sabrina, by email or video, throughout your training.
