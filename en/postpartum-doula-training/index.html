Why a course entirely devoted to the fourth trimester?

Where many doula trainings only skim the postpartum period, Académie Sérénaissance devotes about 35 hours to it. The course was created in the field by Sabrina Chavanel, a doula, helping-relationship counsellor and mother of 5, who lives the fourth trimester alongside families.

It is grounded in safety, rigour and respect for physiology. You learn to recognize warning signs and to apply the "Observe and Refer" principle, so you can support families without ever going beyond your role.

Who is this doula continuing education course for?

It is for you if you are…

  • a certified doula who wants to specialize in postnatal care;
  • a perinatal support person who wants to deepen your knowledge of the fourth trimester;
  • a French speaker in New Brunswick, Quebec, the rest of Canada, France or elsewhere.

Prerequisites

  • Hold a doula diploma or certificate (to upload when you enrol).
  • A computer, tablet or cell phone with Internet access.
  • About 35 hours, spread out at your own pace.

Not a doula yet? This course requires a doula diploma or certificate; the breastfeeding peer supporter training, however, is open to everyone.

What you will be able to do

  • Support matrescence and the postpartum period with confidence.
  • Recognize warning signs and refer at the right time.
  • Support breastfeeding, newborn care and the family circle.
  • Set a clear, ethical professional framework.
  • Move forward with personalized mentoring as needed, by email or video call.

The program: 5 modules, 33 chapters

  1. Physiological, neurobiological and psychosocial foundations · 9 chapters

    Uterine involution and lochia, endocrine transition, pelvic floor, matrescence and the parental brain, perinatal mental health, care pathways, procedures and assistance in New Brunswick, Quebec and France.

  2. Lifestyle, nutrition and non-invasive support protocols · 6 chapters

    The Golden Month, postpartum nutrition, rest, movement and warmth, non-invasive body care and burnout prevention.

  3. Newborn physiology, breastfeeding and parenting support · 8 chapters

    Newborn adaptation and warning signs, breastfeeding, safe sleep, crying and attachment, medical follow-up, first aid and prematurity.

  4. Ethics, legal framework, code of conduct and professional stance · 6 chapters

    Legal framework in France and Quebec, the ANQ code of ethics, confidentiality and client records, starting a business, networking and cultural safety.

  5. Compassionate communication, perinatal grief and closing the fourth trimester · 4 chapters

    Listening, perinatal grief and traumatic births, closing the support relationship, and taking care of yourself as a doula.

An online doula course with assessments and a certificate

Each chapter is read at your own pace, with figures, case studies, printable tools and videos to watch. Assessment is progressive:

  • a practice quiz after each of the 33 chapters (80% to pass);
  • a final exam for each module and a comprehensive final exam of 58 questions;
  • a complete practicum kit and a supervised field case report.

Once you pass, you receive your printed Académie Sérénaissance certificate by mail. This certificate confirms completion of the course: it is neither a regulated professional title nor a diploma recognized by a ministry of education.

The course content is available in English.

Frequently asked questions

What is the format?

Chapters to read at your own pace, on a computer, tablet or cell phone, with figures, case studies, printable tools and videos to watch. Each chapter ends with a practice quiz, and each module with an exam.

How does progression work?

The next chapter opens once you pass the chapter quiz (80%). The module exam opens once all the quizzes are passed, and the next module opens once you pass the exam. Your progress is saved in your account, so you can pick it up on any device.

Will I get support?

Yes! Personalized mentoring is available as needed throughout your course, by email or video call. You can book a time directly from your account.

When do I get access to my course?

After your file (and your diploma for the fourth trimester course) is verified, usually within 24 to 48 hours. You then receive an email, and your courses appear in My account.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, for the fourth trimester course at the regular price ($550): in 2 instalments of $275 or 4 instalments of $137.50. The launch offer price ($250) is paid in a single payment.
